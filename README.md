## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates the use of **Microsoft Excel** to clean, analyze, and visualize customer bike sales data. The goal was to transform raw customer information into an interactive dashboard that highlights purchasing patterns and supports data-driven decision-making.



---
## 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses need to understand which customer characteristics influence bike purchasing decisions. By analyzing demographic and lifestyle data, stakeholders can identify key customer segments and make more informed marketing and sales decisions.

---

## 📊 Dataset

The analysis uses the popular **Bike Sales** dataset, which contains customer information such as:

- Customer Age
- Gender
- Income
- Marital Status
- Education
- Occupation
- Region
- Commute Distance
- Number of Children
- Home Ownership
- Bike Purchase Status

## 📈 Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard enables users to explore bike purchase trends through filters and visualizations, including:

- Average income by bike purchase status
- Customer age group analysis
- Bike purchases by commute distance
- Purchase comparison by gender
- Interactive slicers for dynamic filtering

---

## 📌 Key Insights

The analysis highlights several business insights, including:

- Customers with higher average incomes were generally more likely to purchase bikes.
- Middle-aged customers (Age 31-50) represented the largest proportion of bike purchases.
- Commute distance appeared to influence purchasing behaviour.
- Purchasing patterns varied across customer demographics.
